## What this proves and does not prove

The bounded configured-source capture loop, original preservation, durable
dispatch/indexing, backoff, heartbeat, timed shutdown and concurrent reporting
worked without manual collection actions during this window.

The process started before the later immutable report filename and persistent
model-hold refinements. Those refinements have separate automated regression
tests; this trial must not be described as a live test of them or of model calls.

It does NOT certify two days of operation, crash/reboot recovery, pool renewal,
full national discovery, video OCR/ASR, independent verification, real protocol
totals or election conclusions. No approved live gateway configuration was found.
Newly discovered credentials are not automatically approved. Exhausted or unclear
capacity pauses model work rather than silently using paid/unknown routes.

Next acceptance step: provision an explicit owned/authorized gateway with known
free entitlement and quotas, repeat an end-to-end model-enabled test, add approved
external crash/reboot supervision, then perform the 24/48-hour endurance run.
